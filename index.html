About

A teacher first, for over twelve years.

Sarika Jain has taught commerce to Class 11 and 12 students for more than twelve years. She starts with the concept and moves to practice, so that by exam day the questions already feel familiar.

Through Rhythm Commerce Academy she teaches students online across India and in small batches in Indore, and shares free lectures on YouTube.

"Commerce isn't about memorising. Once a student sees why an entry works, they never forget it."
